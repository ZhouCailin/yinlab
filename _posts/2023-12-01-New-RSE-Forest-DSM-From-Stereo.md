Dr. YIN’s two papers on modeling forest canopy surface retrievals from spaceborne stereogrammetry are published in RSE. Bridging photogrammetry with 3-D ridiative transfer simulation, this study demonstrates our great potential in providing a benchmark for stereo processing algorithms and valuable guidance for future very-high-resolution (VHR) satellite acquisition.

- [Modeling forest canopy surface retrievals using very high-resolution spaceborne stereogrammetry: (I) methods and comparisons with actual data](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0034425723003760#s0005) 
- [Modeling forest canopy surface retrievals using very high-resolution spaceborne stereogrammetry: (II) optimizing acquisition configurations](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0034425723003759).
