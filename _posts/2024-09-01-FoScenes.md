The preliminary version of FoScenes releases 40 seamless scenes over 29 selected sites of various forest types and observation time, with the area ranging from 50 to about 11,000 hectares, using ALS data acquired by NASA Goddard's LiDAR, Hyperspectral & Thermal Imager (G-LiHT).

See more details and access the data [here](www.3msi.net/foscenes)
